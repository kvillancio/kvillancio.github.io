Pawsthetics is a design team at the University of Florida that designs and builds free prostheses for canines in need. I joined the team in my first year at UF and eventually became team captain. During my time on the team, we made prosthetic limbs for three dogs and secured the Donovan J. Welsch Fellowship to help fund our work.


Pawsthetics Instagram

I designed a gesture-based sensor glove with Mia Onadera as a project for an interactive technology class at the University of Washington. We integrated force sensors into a wearable glove and mapped different hand gestures to audio outputs that the user could customize on a mobile app.

I have had the opportunity to learn how to build adaptive technologies for people with disabilities through multiple organizations. I helped customize a ride-on car into a pediatric mobility device through the University of Washington chapter of Go Baby Go. I've also helped customize accessibility tools and teach people how to build accessible switches through hackathons and outreach projects with HuskyADAPT at the University of Washington and adapted accessbile toys through both HuskyADAPT and Generational Relief in Prosthetics (GRiP) at the University of Florida.
